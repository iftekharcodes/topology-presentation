Kahn’s Algorithm

1

Find all sources

Identify all nodes with an indegree of 0 and add them to a ready queue.

2

Remove and update

Pop a node, add to sort, and decrement the indegree of its neighbors.

3

Repeat

If a neighbor's indegree becomes 0, add it to the queue. Continue until empty.

while queue is not empty:
  u = queue.pop()
  result.add(u)
  for v in neighbors(u):
    indegree[v] -= 1
    if indegree[v] == 0:
      queue.push(v)
