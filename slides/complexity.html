Complexity

Time Complexity

O(V + E)

We visit every vertex (V) once and traverse every edge (E) exactly once to update indegrees.

Space Complexity

O(V)

Storing indegrees, the queue, and the final ordering all take space linear to the number of vertices.

Cost Accounting

Graph TraversalO(V)
Edge ReductionsO(E)
Queue OperationsO(V)
Total: O(V + E)
